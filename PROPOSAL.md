**The problem:** 

**The user:** Patients who wear orthokeratology lenses

**What it does:** Outputs how much vision correction is required for 20/20 vision

**Must-have features:** A prompt for users to answer questions, a table where inputs go and output is outputted, a boolean to see if the person is an ortho-k patient or just users who are curious about vision distance

**Nice-to-have features:** An image showing how much correction is needed or a diagram

**The data table:** Data like the prescription inputted and the distance and if they're a patient or not

**The decision it makes:** It doesn't make a decision on it's own, it calculates the amount of vision correction needed using mathematical principles

**The calculation it performs:** The vision correction equation::correction = prescription - (1/distance)
